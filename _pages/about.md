Hi, I’m Punrong Rany, a graduate student, technology professional, and researcher interested in how artificial intelligence can be applied to real-world engineering problems.

I am currently pursuing an MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering at University of Calgary, where my work focuses on the intersection of AI, machine learning, software engineering, and energy systems. I am particularly interested in building AI systems that are not only accurate, but also reliable, interpretable, and useful in real-world environments.

Before starting my graduate studies, I worked as a Project Manager at Canadia Bank, where I worked on technology and digital transformation initiatives across areas including digital banking, payments, IT security, and merchant solutions. Working closely with technical teams, business stakeholders, and large-scale technology projects gave me a practical perspective on how software and AI need to work beyond the laboratory.

My academic background is interdisciplinary. I hold a Master of Science in Innovation Management & Entrepreneurship from Temple University, as well as bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and International Studies from the Royal University of Phnom Penh. I was also fortunate to be selected as a Fulbright Scholar, an experience that shaped my interest in using technology to solve meaningful problems and work across disciplines.

Research Interests
======
My current interests are broadly centered around AI for real-world systems, particularly:
- Machine learning for energy and power systems
- AI reliability and generalization
- Explainable and interpretable AI  
- Reinforcement learning and intelligent agents
- Computer vision and human-centered AI  
- AI systems that can support decision-making in complex environments

I am especially interested in questions such as: How can we make AI systems more reliable when they encounter situations they have not seen before? How can we understand why an AI model makes a particular decision? And how can we design AI systems that are useful in safety- and reliability-critical applications such as energy systems?

From Technology to Research
======
My journey has moved across several different areas of technology, from computer science and entrepreneurship to project management and now engineering research.

That combination has shaped how I approach problems. I enjoy understanding both the technical side of a problem and the practical context in which a solution will actually be used.

I am still early in my research journey, and one of my goals during my MSc is to develop a deeper understanding of machine learning while learning how to turn real-world engineering problems into meaningful research questions.

Outside of research, I enjoy exploring new places, hiking, learning new technologies, and documenting the things I learn along the way.

This website is a space where I share my research, projects, academic work, and things I learn throughout the journey.
